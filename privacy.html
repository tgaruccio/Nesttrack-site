Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

NestTrack (“NestTrack,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a real estate productivity platform operated by Thomas Garuccio LLC. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email us at hi@nesttrack.io.

Who this policy covers

NestTrack is used by real estate professionals (“Agents”) to run their business, and by the clients and contacts those Agents invite into the platform (“Clients”). This policy applies to both. Agents are responsible for the contact information they enter about their own clients and for having the right to share it with us for processing.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, brokerage, profile photo, and similar details you provide when you sign up or set up your profile.
  • Contact and client information — Agents store information about their clients and contacts in NestTrack, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, transaction details, notes, and communication history. This information is entered and controlled by the Agent.
  • Payment information — when you subscribe, our payment processor collects your billing details. We do not store full card numbers ourselves (see “Service providers” below).
  • Messages and content — messages, documents, and other content you create or upload in the platform.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — basic information about how you use NestTrack, such as pages visited and features used, to operate and improve the service.
  • Device and notification data — if you enable push notifications, we store a notification token so we can deliver alerts to your device.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the NestTrack platform;
  • Let Agents manage their clients, deals, showings, and follow-up;
  • Send transactional emails, text messages, and push notifications on an Agent’s behalf or to keep users informed (for example, a status update to a client, or a new-lead alert to an Agent);
  • Process subscriptions and payments;
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you about the service;
  • Protect the security and integrity of the platform and comply with legal obligations.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run the service:

Between Agents and their Clients

NestTrack is a tool that connects Agents with their clients. Information an Agent enters or generates is visible within that Agent’s account and, where the Agent chooses, to the relevant Client (for example, a client-facing timeline or status update).

Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate NestTrack. Each receives only the information needed to perform its function:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Google Firebase — secure data storage, authentication, and hosting.
  • OneSignal — delivery of push notifications.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional and branded email.
  • Cloudflare — runs the NestTrack server and stores photos and videos an Agent uploads for social posts.

These providers process information on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Apps and AI assistants you connect

An Agent can choose to connect their NestTrack account to an AI assistant such as Claude, made by Anthropic. This only happens when the Agent signs in to NestTrack and approves the connection. While connected, the assistant can read the records in that Agent’s own account (clients and contacts, notes, deals, appointments, to-dos and follow-ups) and can add or update records when the Agent asks it to. It cannot send email, text messages or notifications to Clients.

Information the assistant reads is sent to the assistant’s provider to answer the Agent’s request and is handled under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy and the Agent’s account with them. NestTrack does not record the Agent’s conversations with the assistant. We store only what the Agent asks the assistant to save in NestTrack, such as a note, a to-do, an appointment or a daily briefing. An Agent can disconnect at any time from within the assistant, and access ends when they do.

Social media accounts you connect

An Agent can choose to connect their own social media accounts to NestTrack so they can write a post once and publish or schedule it from NestTrack. A connection is only made when the Agent signs in on that platform’s own page and approves it. NestTrack never sees or stores the Agent’s password for any of these platforms.

What we receive and store. For each connected account we store its name or username, the identifier the platform gives it, and the access keys the platform issues so that NestTrack can post to it. Access keys are encrypted before they are stored and are never sent to the Agent’s browser. We do not collect followers, friends lists, private messages, or viewing and browsing activity from any of these platforms.

How we use it. Only to show the Agent which accounts are connected and to publish the posts that the Agent creates and approves, at the time the Agent chooses. NestTrack does not post on its own, and we do not use this information for advertising or sell or share it with anyone else.

Photos, videos and captions. Media an Agent uploads for a post is stored so the platform can retrieve it. To make that possible the file is given a private web address that is not listed anywhere. Files are deleted about seven days after the post goes out, or when the Agent deletes the draft or cancels the post. The caption, the time, and whether the post succeeded are kept in the Agent’s account until the Agent removes them. Once something is published, it is also held by the platform it was published to, under that platform’s terms.

  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta) — when an Agent connects a Facebook Page or an Instagram professional account, we receive the Page or account name and identifier, the identifier of the person who approved the connection, and access keys for publishing. We use them to publish the Agent’s posts to that Page or account. Meta handles information under the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok — when an Agent connects a TikTok account, we receive the account’s identifier, display name and username, and access keys. When the Agent writes a post we also ask TikTok for that account’s profile picture and posting options (for example who is allowed to view a post) so the Agent can choose them; these are shown to the Agent and are not stored. We use the connection to publish the Agent’s videos to their profile, or to send a video to their TikTok inbox as a draft. TikTok handles information under the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube (Google) — where an Agent connects a YouTube channel, NestTrack uses YouTube API Services. We receive the channel name and identifier and access keys, and use them to upload the Agent’s videos to that channel. By connecting a YouTube channel, the Agent also agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles information under the Google Privacy Policy. NestTrack’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting. An Agent can remove a connected account at any time on the Social page in NestTrack. Removing it deletes the access keys we hold for it and, where the platform supports it, tells the platform to end NestTrack’s access. Access can also be removed on the platform itself: in Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations, in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, and for Google at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To have everything we hold about a connected account deleted, email hi@nesttrack.io. Removing a connection does not take down posts that were already published; those are deleted on the platform itself.

Legal reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or NestTrack.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Agents may delete client records within the platform. If you close your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal, accounting, or security reasons.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — you can view and update your profile information in the app at any time.
  • Notifications — you can turn push notifications on or off through your device settings.
  • Deletion — you may request deletion of your personal information by emailing hi@nesttrack.io. If you are a Client whose information was added by an Agent, we may direct your request to that Agent, who controls the record.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email above.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and reputable infrastructure providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your data and limit access to it.

Children

NestTrack is a business tool intended for real estate professionals and their adult clients. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated through the platform or by email where appropriate.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email hi@nesttrack.io.
