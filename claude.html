NestTrack for Claude

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Connect your NestTrack account to Claude and work your business by asking. Claude can look things up in your NestTrack and add or update your own records. It never contacts your clients.

What you need

  • A NestTrack agent or lender account
  • A Claude account

How to connect

  1. In Claude, open the connectors directory and search for NestTrack.
  2. Choose Connect.
  3. Sign in to NestTrack when the page opens, then choose Allow.

You only do this once. To disconnect, remove NestTrack from your connectors in Claude.

Things to try

“What’s on my plate today?”

“Who am I overdue to follow up with?”

“Add a note to Jordan Lee: wants a first-floor bedroom.”

“Book a listing appointment with the Parkers on Tuesday at 6pm.”

“Which deals close this month?”

What Claude will not do

  • Contact your clients. It cannot send an email, a text or a notification. Appointments it creates are saved without a client email. Send that from NestTrack if you want one sent.
  • See anyone else’s records. It only sees the account you signed in with.
  • Delete clients or deals. It can delete a to-do and cancel an appointment, and Claude asks you first.

Troubleshooting

  • Claude says to reconnect NestTrack. Your sign-in expired. Remove the connector and connect again.
  • Claude says your time zone is not saved. Open NestTrack once on your phone or computer, then ask again.
  • Claude says a contact already exists. Someone with that email or phone is already in your NestTrack. Ask Claude to update that person instead.
  • A transaction task will not check off. Mark it done on the deal in NestTrack so the deal’s checklist updates too.
  • Claude cannot change a client’s email. That client has a portal login under their current email. Change it on their card in NestTrack.

Your data

The connector reads and writes only the records in your own NestTrack account. It stores what you ask Claude to save, such as a note, a to-do, an appointment or a briefing, and nothing else from your conversation. See our privacy policy.
