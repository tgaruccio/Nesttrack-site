6 Retain · After the keys

The day after closing, most agents vanish. You won't.

Nearly 70–80% of a seasoned agent's business comes from past clients and referrals — yet the years between deals are exactly where those relationships go cold. NestTracker keeps you in the picture on autopilot. Every closed client gets an ongoing home dashboard in your brand: live value and equity, milestone alerts (when they cross into no-PMI territory, when a refi finally pencils, when they've built real move-up money), and seasonal maintenance reminders that recommend your trusted pros. Plus a walkthrough capture and a running home file — appliances, systems, warranties — so you're the one they text when anything comes up. You stay the trusted advisor for years, without lifting a finger.
